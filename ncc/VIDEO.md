# Demo video — script + shot list

3 minutes. Shoot on site at membrane stage, plus screen capture. Edit the
night before submission; reserve the full day.

## The arc (say this, in this order)

1. **The leak (0:00–0:25).** Stained ceiling / failed bathroom photo.
   Voiceover: "Waterproofing is the most common serious defect in NSW
   apartments. 39% of new strata buildings have serious defects; nearly
   two-thirds of those are waterproofing. Then the tiles go on and nobody
   sees the membrane again until it leaks."
2. **Whose problem (0:25–0:45).** Membrane-stage bathroom, waterproofer at
   work. "This is for the waterproofer, not the inspector. When it leaks in
   three years, you get blamed and you have nothing to prove you did it
   right. This is your evidence."
3. **Point the phone (0:45–1:30).** Live: open Jarvis, load the site photo,
   SAM boxes appear, tap "shower hob" → correct 3D detail with the clause.
   "See how it should be built, before you build it."
4. **Built wrong (1:30–2:00).** Flip the violation toggle. Geometry shortens
   AND the check turns red. "The 3D is generated from the rule numbers —
   feed it a new manual and you get new details. Nothing here was modelled
   by hand."
5. **Evidence (2:00–2:30).** Tap Capture evidence → PNG with timestamp +
   rule ids. "Your proof, taken before anything covered it."
6. **Close (2:30–3:00).** Benchmark chart: cold 35%, grounded 100%.
   "Chatbots guess. This reads the manual. And waterproofing is just the
   first library — any installation guide becomes a detail library."

## What the video must NOT claim

- No measurement ("it measured 20mm") — the tool shows what correct looks
  like; it does not measure the real install.
- No compliance verdict ("your bathroom passes") — shows the detail, not a
  grade. The capture PNG says this on its face; keep it in frame.
